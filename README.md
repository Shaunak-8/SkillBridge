# SkillBridge – Local Business to Student Project Matching Platform

> Bridging the gap between local businesses facing real-world challenges and students looking for practical experience.

## 📌 Problem Statement

**Problem Statement 24: Local Business Problem-to-Student Project Matching Platform**

Local businesses face everyday challenges but often lack the technical expertise, resources, or connections to solve them. Meanwhile, students possess valuable skills but struggle to find meaningful real-world projects.

Currently, there is no streamlined platform that helps businesses define their problems, connect with suitable student teams, and track project outcomes.

## 💡 Our Solution

**SkillBridge** is an AI-powered platform that connects local businesses with skilled students through real-world projects.

Businesses can post their challenges, students can discover relevant opportunities, and AI helps simplify project creation, skill-based matching, and candidate evaluation.

The platform aims to create a mutually beneficial ecosystem where:

- **Businesses** receive affordable, innovative solutions.
- **Students** gain practical experience, portfolio projects, and industry exposure.
- **Colleges** can encourage experiential learning and industry collaboration.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🏢 For Businesses

- **Business Registration:** Create and manage business profiles.
- **AI-Powered Project Scoping:** Convert vague business problems into structured project briefs.
- **Project Posting:** Publish projects with required skills, duration, and expected deliverables.
- **Smart Student Matching:** Discover students based on relevant skills and interests.
- **AI Interview (Premium):** Evaluate shortlisted candidates through AI-generated interviews.
- **Project Tracking:** Monitor progress, milestones, and deliverables.
- **Feedback & Reviews:** Evaluate completed projects and student performance.

### 🎓 For Students

- **Student Profiles:** Showcase skills, education, interests, and experience.
- **Resume Upload:** Upload resumes for skill extraction and profile enhancement.
- **AI-Based Matching:** Receive project recommendations based on skills and interests.
- **Project Applications:** Apply to relevant business projects.
- **Skill-Based Assessments:** Demonstrate relevant technical or practical knowledge.
- **Portfolio Building:** Showcase completed projects and achievements.
- **Project Dashboard:** Track applications, ongoing projects, and completed work.

### 🤖 AI-Powered Features

**1. AI Project Brief Generator**

Converts informal business requirements into actionable project descriptions containing:

- Problem definition
- Required skills
- Expected deliverables
- Estimated timeline
- Project scope

**2. AI Resume Analysis**

Analyzes uploaded resumes to identify relevant skills and experience, helping match students with suitable projects.

**3. AI Matching Engine**

Recommends student candidates using:

- Skill compatibility
- Project requirements
- Student interests
- Experience level
- Availability

**4. AI Interview System (Premium Feature)**

Businesses can optionally use AI interviews to evaluate applicants.

The system can:

- Generate role-specific interview questions.
- Conduct structured candidate assessments.
- Evaluate answers against predefined criteria.
- Generate interview summaries and evaluation scores.
- Help businesses shortlist suitable candidates.

AI scores are intended to support human decision-making rather than automatically reject candidates.

## 🔄 Platform Workflow

1. **Business Registration:** A business signs up and creates its profile.
2. **Problem Submission:** The business describes a challenge in simple language.
3. **AI Project Generation:** AI transforms the challenge into a structured project brief.
4. **Project Publishing:** The business reviews and publishes the project.
5. **Student Matching:** The platform recommends suitable students.
6. **Student Applications:** Interested students apply with their profiles and resumes.
7. **Candidate Evaluation:** Businesses review applications and optionally conduct AI interviews.
8. **Project Execution:** Selected students collaborate with the business.
9. **Outcome Tracking:** Milestones, deliverables, and feedback are recorded.
10. **Project Completion:** Students gain verified project experience and businesses receive solutions.

## 💰 Revenue Model

SkillBridge follows a **freemium business model**.

**Free Features**
- Business and student registration
- Basic project posting
- Student applications
- Basic skill-based matching
- Project management

**Premium Features for Businesses**
- AI-powered candidate interviews
- Detailed candidate evaluation reports
- Advanced candidate filtering
- Enhanced shortlisting tools

This allows students to access opportunities while businesses pay for optional advanced recruitment tools.

## 🔐 Privacy & Security
